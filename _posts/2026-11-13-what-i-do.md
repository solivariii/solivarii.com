I'm Gunnar Bachelor, and I'm an IT automation consultant. My work is simple to describe: I find the parts of running your IT that people do by hand, over and over, and I turn them into automation that does the job the same way every time.

## The problem

Most teams don't have an automation problem. They have a time problem that looks like a lot of small chores:

- A new hire waits two days for accounts, because setting them up is a checklist someone has to work through.
- Deployments happen on Friday afternoons, by hand, and only one person knows all the steps.
- Backups exist, probably, but nobody has tried restoring one.
- Servers were set up years ago and nobody is quite sure how, so nobody wants to touch them.

Each of these is a few minutes or a few hours. Together they add up to a part-time job, and they're exactly where outages and security gaps come from.

## What I automate

- **Provisioning and configuration.** Servers, cloud resources and services defined as code, so they can be rebuilt, reviewed and changed safely.
- **Deployments.** Push a change, and it ships. No checklists, no "it works on my machine".
- **Backups and recovery.** Scheduled, off-site, and tested, so a restore is a routine operation rather than a crisis.
- **Accounts and access.** Onboarding and offboarding that happen in one step, with access removed when people leave.
- **Routine maintenance.** Updates, certificate renewals, monitoring and alerts that run without anyone having to remember them.
- **Glue work.** Small scripts and integrations that move data between the tools you already use.

## How I work

I start by reading what you already have and asking which tasks cost you the most time or worry. Then I automate the ones worth automating, one at a time, in plain, well-known tools rather than anything exotic.

Everything I build comes with documentation and is handed over to you. The goal is that your team can understand it, run it and change it without me.
